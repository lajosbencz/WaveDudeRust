# Code of Conduct

We are here to build quality software, act accordingly.

- **No personal attacks, threats or harassment.**
  They waste everyone's time and end your participation.
  Argue with the code, never with the person.
- **The physics decides.**
  Claims come with a measurement or a reference.
- **Be thorough.**
  A contribution arrives finished: tested and documented.
- **Don't panic.**
  Ask when unsure, and say how/what you tried.
  A precise question gets a precise answer.

## Reporting

Report breaches privately through [the private form](https://github.com/lajosbencz/WaveDudeRust/security/advisories/new).

## Reservations

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block contributors who break these rules.
